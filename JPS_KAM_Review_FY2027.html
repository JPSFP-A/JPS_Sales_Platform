Sharmica Holness's portfolio falls 74.5 GWh and needs discussion in the session. Three accounts account for effectively all of it: Alcoa Minerals (−53.0 GWh), which completed its move to self-generation in August 2026; MBJ Airports (−14.5), carried at a 60% solar offset for eleven months of FY2027; and Windalco (−10.7), reduced after its turbines went offline late in 2026 and not assumed to recover. None is a performance issue, all three are structural, but the portfolio target should be reset rather than left to show a 58% decline against an unchanged prior year.

Petrojam. Modelled as a temporary shutdown rather than a step-down: 50% of trend in January and February, back to trend in March, 10% above trend April to June as the plant catches up, and back to trend from July. Net effect is 1.1 GWh below trend in FY2027 and nil in FY2028. The stored monthly percentages are solved values, not face values, because the engine applies each driver against a rolling three-month base. Entering a flat −50% would compound to a 58% drop in the second month and leave the base depressed for months afterwards. The KAM recorded 77 days; the forecast holds 70 as the conservative read.

Points for the Session

  • Kingston Free Port Terminal has no key account manager assigned. It bills 25.3 GWh, is forecast to grow to 28.4, and sits on the register as a possible defection with no quantified impact and nobody owning the relationship. This is the largest open item in the pack, and it needs an owner before it needs a number.
  • MBJ Airports, appears twice, once confirmed for eleven months and once as possible for April. The 4.32 GWh a year in the supporting note does not reconcile to a 60% offset, which would need roughly 9 GWh. Sharmica Holness to confirm which is right; roughly 5 GWh of FY2027 turns on it.
  • Sharmica Holness's portfolio target, down 57% on two structural losses. Reset the target rather than carrying an unachievable comparative.
  • Independence Park, booked at +250% for December 2029, outside the forecast horizon. Confirm whether any load arrives earlier in the construction programme.
  • Three unquantified small possibles: Woolworth, Rock Solid and Contax360 are all placeholders at zero impact. Contax360 already bills nothing. Either quantify or close them.
  • Solar is now a commercial trend, not just residential: Sagicor at three sites, Grace Food Processors, Rainforest Seafood, Sutherland and Clarendon Distillers all in FY2027. Worth a coordinated view rather than account-by-account treatment.
